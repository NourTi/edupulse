# Education Product Benchmark Notes

## Teach ’n Go: Arabic school-management positioning

Teach ’n Go presents Arabic support alongside central student records, scheduling, teacher management, attendance, payments, and receipts. Its message is operational clarity for multilingual staff, students, and families rather than a standalone teacher tool. Source: https://www.teachngo.com/language-pages/arabic

**Implication for EduPulse:** Arabic must be a working language for forms, operational states, messages, and documents, not a superficial translation toggle. Student records, attendance, timetables, payments/receipts, and institution-family visibility are baseline expectations.

## Kinderpedia: full-school operating surface

Kinderpedia positions a school-management system around consolidated school data, planning/reporting, attendance, grades and feedback, tuition payment notifications, parent communication, staff roles, family access, and progress assessment. Source: https://www.kinderpedia.co/en/complete-school-management-software

**Implication for EduPulse:** The product should make the value chain visible: an administrator sees operational status and controls; a teacher sees cohorts, attendance, subjects, and feedback; a student/guardian sees approved progress, reports, balances, and communication. The product must include strong parent communication, learner progress by subject, role-specific visibility, and report/receipt output.

## Scope added to the prototype

1. Arabic-first registration form with guardian, class, academic year, and subject selection.
2. Three role modes: administrator, teacher, and student, with tailored dashboards.
3. Core school subjects plus language-centre and CEFR learning pathways.
4. CEFR skill tracking with evidence-aware level summary.
5. Guardian communication drafts and student progress report view.
6. Local payment records and printable receipt generation.
7. A scrollable product page explaining roles, modules, privacy, and workflows rather than a single cinematic hero.

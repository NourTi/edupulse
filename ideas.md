# EduPulse Design Ground Truth

## Reference Design: Cinematic Local-First Console

The supplied design specification is the ground-truth visual system for the first EduPulse build. The application must use its **fullscreen looping video**, deep-navy palette, minimal glassmorphic treatment, Instrument Serif display typography, Inter body typography, centered cinematic hero composition, thin luminous liquid-glass outlines, and restrained fade-rise entry motion exactly where those elements are specified.

### Design Movement

Editorial cinematic minimalism translated into an education operations console. The public welcome layer is deliberately quiet and spacious; the active workspace inherits the same nocturnal navy, serif-led hierarchy, glass surfaces, and uncluttered visual rhythm.

### Core Principles

1. Preserve the supplied video as the sole source of hero depth; do not add blobs, radial gradients, or synthetic overlays.
2. Use the supplied liquid-glass recipe for the navigation and primary calls to action; do not replace it with generic translucent cards.
3. Use Instrument Serif for branded display language and Inter for operational data, controls, tables, and body copy.
4. Keep the composition calm: sparse typography, wide negative space, slim separators, and direct task visibility.

### Color Philosophy

Deep navy is the enduring, privacy-oriented foundation. White indicates the current action or trusted information; the supplied muted gray holds secondary context. Operational status uses narrow, accessible accents only when meaning requires it.

### Layout Paradigm

The entry route is a vertically centered cinematic threshold. The app workspace uses a full-bleed screen with a quiet left rail and an editorial content field rather than a conventional card-grid dashboard.

### Signature Elements

1. The supplied looping video at full bleed on the welcome view.
2. Luminous, border-only liquid-glass actions with a slight inner highlight.
3. Large serif lines paired with compact, low-contrast operational metadata.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions are precise and measured. The system surfaces what needs attention without urgency theatrics. Buttons compress subtly on press; menus and navigation remain fast and clear.

### Animation

Use the supplied `fade-rise` sequence for the welcome hierarchy. Use opacity and transform transitions only, keep routine interface motion under 250ms, and honor reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System

Instrument Serif is the display typeface for the EduPulse wordmark, welcome statement, major section titles, and empty-state messages. Inter 400/500 serves controls, table data, helper text, and system status.

### Brand Essence

EduPulse is a private, local-first operating space for educators who need calm control over complex student relationships.

**Personality:** discreet, literary, exacting.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are calm and declarative. CTAs name the next meaningful action rather than using generic onboarding language.

Examples: “A clear day for every learner.” and “Open the records that need you.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The wordmark is **EduPulse** in Instrument Serif with a small superscript local-first dot mark. The product mark is a high-contrast vertical pulse formed by three narrow book-spine lines, rendered without text.

### Signature Brand Color

**Midnight Slate — #00364A**, derived from the supplied `201 100% 13%` background value.

## Style Decisions

- The welcome headline remains a single ivory Instrument Serif statement; muted gray is reserved for metadata and supporting copy.
- Public navigation and calls to action name specific local-record and workspace actions rather than generic marketing actions.
- The welcome layer includes compact record and task metadata with slim rules to assert EduPulse as a private operational console while preserving the supplied video-first composition.
- Arabic display language uses Amiri as the editorial counterpart to Instrument Serif; operational labels retain the neutral Inter-style sans hierarchy.
- Below the hero, role, subject, and privacy content is treated as a slim-rule register rather than a repeated rounded-card feature grid.

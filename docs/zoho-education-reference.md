# Zoho Education CRM Reference Review

## Reference

The user-provided reference is [Zoho CRM for education](https://www.zoho.com/crm/verticals/education/). The page presents an education CRM around one operational story: manage the full school database, improve admissions and follow-up, and simplify daily operations.

## Useful patterns to adapt

| Reference pattern | EduPulse adaptation |
|---|---|
| A prominent “entire school database” story | Make the dashboard immediately show the school-wide operational picture: learners, staff, attendance, payments, CRM work, and recent actions. |
| Clear stakeholder scope | Keep role-aware navigation for administrators, teachers, students, guardians, registrars, finance staff, and counsellors. |
| Portal separation and permission framing | Surface role-specific quick actions and preserve institution-scoped authorization; do not expose cross-tenant data. |
| Admissions and conversion workflow | Adapt to Algerian education operations: registration pipeline, learner status, follow-ups, programmes, and school-stage context rather than sales-first language. |
| Automation and sequential workflows | Present CRM tasks, essay review, behaviour follow-up, mentorship, resources, language milestones, and client follow-ups as actionable work queues. |
| Analytics and reports | Use compact KPI cards, trend summaries, activity feed, and module shortcuts that can later be backed by live analytics. |
| Bright, high-contrast presentation | Replace the current heavy dark-only mood with a light warm canvas, vivid indigo/teal/amber/coral accents, white surfaces, and selective dark text panels. |

## Design direction

EduPulse should borrow the reference’s clarity and operational confidence, not its brand, copy, or proprietary visual assets. The adapted interface will remain Arabic-first and RTL. It should feel like a calm school operations cockpit: vivid enough to be inviting, restrained enough for long work sessions, and explicit about what needs attention.

The main dashboard should lead with a welcome/status area, a colorful KPI row, a “school database” module grid, a priority work queue, recent activity, and an Algeria-specific education pathway summary. The landing page can use brighter feature sections and light cards while retaining the existing EduPulse identity and morphism badges.

## System Context (CBA since 2003/2004)
- Law N°08-04 (23 Jan 2008): Conseil National des Programmes (CNP) overhauls all disciplines. Competency-Based Approach (APC) from 2003/2004.
- English curriculum is **CB** (Competency-Based), focused on outcomes/outputs, linking school ↔ real life (Auerbach/Richards & Rodgers).
- CBA characteristics: learner-centred, task/performance oriented, outcomes explicit a priori, **continuous ongoing assessment**, individualized, constructivist (build own knowledge).
- Teacher role: facilitator/mentor/adviser/resource person. Learner role: goal-setting, self-assessment, reflective.
- Assessment: portfolio important; formative (helps see progress, difficulties) + summative/certifying (judge degree, progression decision). Situation d'Intégration is strategy to achieve competency.

## Finalités 3AS (English)
- Consolidate & develop bases from cycle moyen.
- Four skills integrated: Listening/Speaking/Reading/Writing around **interacting orally, interpreting messages, producing written messages**.
- By end of 3AS: produce descriptive, narrative, argumentative, expositive or injunctive passage of **20 lines** correctly, using oral/written support.
- Three primary competencies to master at end 3AS (see New Prospects):
  1. Interacting orally
  2. Interpreting messages
  3. Producing written messages (types above)
- Entry profile: what learner has before 3AS; Exit profile: attained at end of secondary.
- Resources: savoirs (lexis/grammar/phonology), savoir-faire (skills), attitudes (active citizenship, ethics).

## Streams & Unit Selection
- **Lettres & Philosophie + Langues Étrangères:** Units 1,2,3,6
- **Sciences Exp / Math / Tech Math / Gestion & Économie:** Units 2,3,4,5 (varies by year; 4 mandatory units chosen with inspector/teacher)
- Themes in New Prospects (200? edition): A. Ancient civilizations B. Ethics in business C. Education in world: comparing educational systems D. Advertising, consumers and safety E. Astronomy and solar system F. Feelings and emotions

## Integration Principle
- After 3/4 weeks teaching, learners must be trained to **mobilize resources and reinvest in problem-solving situation** (pair/group project work) — this is the Situation d'Intégration.
- Project outcomes per unit (e.g., U2: write a charter of ethics in business; U4: make survey on impact of advertising)

# Progression Annuelle — Anglais 3AS (September 2018, MEN)
Source: 3as.ency-education.com — Annual Progression - Secondary, Ministry of National Education

## General Instructions
1. Assessment should occur at **regular intervals during the sequence and at end of sequence** + designated exam periods.
2. After 3/4 weeks of teaching, learners must be trained on how to **mobilize resources and reinvest in problem-solving situation** via pair/group work.
- Pre-requisites are **in bold** — teachers must check assimilation via diagnostic test at start of each unit and regulate teaching accordingly.

## Semester 1

### Unit 1 — Exploring the Past: Ancient Civilizations
- **Time:** ~28h total for Interacting/Interpreting/Producing. Ancient civs (Egypt, etc.) 5 major civs, rise/fall, contributions, past habits.
- **Activities:** filling spidergram about achievements in Islamic civ, completing network tree, drawing wheel of civs chronologically.
- **Grammar:** Expressing concession (though, in spite of, but, however...), Past simple/past perfect, etc.

### Unit 2 — Ill-Gotten Gains Never Prosper: Ethics in Business
- **Theme:** Fraud, corruption, counterfeiting, ethics.
- **Project outcome:** Writing a **charter of ethics in business**
- **Time:** 7 weeks / 21-24h (sources vary: 7w 21h, 8w 24h)
- **Objectives SWBAT:** speak/write about social auditing and ethics; define ethics in business; consider honesty as active citizenship; discuss counterfeiting.
- **WBA To:** use conditional + should/had better to give advice; consider honesty; use conjunctions to express cause/effect (so, thus, consequently; so+adj+that, such+noun+that); express obligation/necessity (must/have to); review passive with modals; formation of nouns/opposites.
- **Activities:** Interpreting picture (unethical practice), gap-filling definitions, writing public statement about counterfeiting, filling word map fraud, writing opinion article, debate on importance of ethics, using guidelines/diagram/checklist to write policy statement for investment fund, responding to text on social auditing.
- **Phonology:** Pronouncing ethics (singular vs plural), stress in -ics words penultimate (statistics).
- **Tasks:** Agreement/disagreement dialogues, letters of advice.

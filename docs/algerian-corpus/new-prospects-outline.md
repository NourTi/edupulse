# New Prospects — 3AS English Textbook Outline (Seed)
Source: eddirasa Book_Pro_Anglais_3AS + salemzemali lesson plans + SSRN authenticity study

## Structure
- 270 pages divided into two sequences, six units. Each unit ends with **Project**.
- Themes:
  1. **Ancient Civilization (Exploring the Past)**
  2. **Ethics in Business (Ill-Gotten Gains Never Prosper)** — ethics, fraud, corruption, counterfeiting, social auditing, conditional/should/had better, cause/effect.
  3. **Education in the World: Comparing Educational Systems** — systems, unless/if not, advice/obligation, public vs private school.
  4. **Advertising, Consumers and Safety (Safety First)** — advertising, may/might/could, consumer safety, survey.
  5. **Astronomy and the Solar System (It's a Giant Leap for Mankind)**
  6. **Feelings and Emotions (We Are a Family)** — humour, emotions, friendship, present/past simple, gerund, I'd rather.

## Stream Mapping (choose 4 mandatory units with inspector)
- **Literature & Philosophy + Foreign Languages:** Units 1,2,3,6
- **Experimental Science, Math, Tech Math, Management & Economy:** Units 2,3,4,5 (some sources say 2,3,4,6 — verify with inspector; textbook says each stream chooses 4 most related)

## Authenticity Caveat (research finding)
- Study of 35 teachers (70% 15-20y exp): textbook lacks authentic language, tasks mechanical, poor representation, not enough communicative competence. 90% rely on textbook but 95% prefer authentic materials (audio-visual, posters, songs). Teachers need yearly workshops.
- Implication for EduPulse: **Do not rely solely on textbook for lesson plan.** Our RAG must supplement with authentic tasks (dialogues expressing suggestion/apology/offer where tasks are genuine).
